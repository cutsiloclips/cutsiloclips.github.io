Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Cutsilo Clips ("we") publishes short gaming video clips on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. To do that we use a private publishing tool that connects to those platforms through their official APIs. This policy explains what that tool handles.

What the tool accesses

The tool signs in only to Cutsilo Clips' own accounts, through each platform's official sign-in (OAuth). With that permission it:

  • uploads videos and their titles, captions and hashtags to our own accounts;
  • reads basic information about our own accounts (name, handle, account type) to confirm the connection;
  • reads performance statistics of the videos we published (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time) to learn which clips work.

It does not access other people's accounts, private messages, contact lists or follower identities, and it does not collect personal data about viewers.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and statistics are stored on our own systems, protected by access controls, and used only to run Cutsilo Clips. We do not sell or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Video analysis and caption writing use AI services (Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude). They receive only the gameplay footage being clipped and short text descriptions of it, never platform account data.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected. Statistics are kept while the clips stay published. Access can be withdrawn at any time in each platform's security settings, for example Google Account > Security > Third-party connections, or the "Apps and websites" settings on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

Content and removal requests

Clips use our own gameplay or footage used with the creator's permission. To ask for a clip to be removed, or for any question about this policy, write to cutsiloclips@gmail.com. We answer within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated.
