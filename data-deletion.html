Data Deletion

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The Cutsilo Clips publishing tool signs in only to Cutsilo Clips' own accounts and stores no data about other people. If you connected an account to it, or want any data related to you removed, you can do it in two ways.

Remove access yourself

  • Facebook: Settings > Security and login > Business integrations (or Apps and websites), then remove "Cutsilo Clips Publisher".
  • Instagram: Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites, then remove "Cutsilo Clips Publisher".
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services, then remove the app.
  • Google / YouTube: Google Account > Security > Third-party connections, then remove the app.

When access is removed, the stored access token stops working and is deleted at the next sync.

Ask us to delete data

Write to cutsiloclips@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We delete the tokens and statistics linked to your account, and any clip you ask us to remove, within 7 days and confirm by email.
